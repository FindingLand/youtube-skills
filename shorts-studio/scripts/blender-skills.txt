Claude Code can now build 3D models in Blender for you.
It's called Blender Skills, a free pack of 94 skills with over four thousand stars on GitHub.
Each skill teaches Claude one job a 3D artist does.
Modeling, materials, lighting, rigging, animation, even export to Unity, Unreal and Godot.
You connect Blender to Claude once, with a free add-on called Blender MCP.
Then you just describe what you want. Say, a low poly sci-fi rifle for a game.
A director skill reads your request and picks the right skills, in order.
Model, then UVs, then materials, then export. Claude does the work inside Blender.
You can even attach a photo, and it builds a match.
But the skills are open source and free. Link's in the description.
